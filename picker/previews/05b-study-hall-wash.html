Why pastel works

1

Calm authority

Soft color lowers resistance without losing structure. The deck feels considered, not loud.

2

Clear hierarchy

Pastel fills separate ideas while ink text keeps every label crisp and legible.

3

Human warmth

Color signals approachability, even in formal rooms where trust is the point.
