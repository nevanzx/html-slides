Sugar Grid / Principles
04 / 13
Key points

Four rules we design by

Every decision in this deck follows one of these. When two options look equally good, the rule decides.

01

Color earns its place

Every pastel has a job. Pink pulls attention, sky explains, lime confirms, tangerine points forward.

02

Structure before decoration

The grid holds the page. Shapes sit on top of it — never in place of it.

03

Type is the loudest element

Archivo 900 carries the message. Everything else supports it and stays quiet.

04

Keep the edge crisp

2px rules, flat fills, sharp geometry. No soft shadows, no gradients, no haze.

Grid

Every layout is measured in 64px steps. If a gap feels random, it probably is.

Sugar Grid / Color
05 / 13
Color system

Color with a job to do

Four pastels, one ink, one white. Each color owns a role so a page never argues with itself.

Candy Pink — #FFD1E3

The attention color. Headline highlights and the primary call to action.

Sky — #CDE7FF

The explainer. Diagrams, secondary panels, and supporting data.

Lime — #E4F5C1

The confirmation. Positive states, checklists, and success moments.

Tangerine — #FFE0C2

The directional color. Arrows, next steps, and forward motion.

Sugar Grid / By the numbers
06 / 13
By the numbers

Small system, big range

A tight set of constants is what makes the output feel consistent at speed.

64px
Base unit

Every margin, gap, and grid line is a multiple of 64.

4
Pastels

One color, one job. Never two accents fighting for the same point.

12
Columns

A single grid under every layout, from cover to closing slide.

2px
Rules

One line weight for every border, divider, and edge in the system.

In practice

Shapes that carry meaning

Three primitives cover every decorative need in the system.

Fig. 01 — Geometry set

Circle, arch, and quarter-round. Always flat, always cropped by the frame.

Circle

Community and continuity. Used for avatars, stat marks, and soft anchors.

Arch

Openness. Frames a figure without closing it, and lifts a quiet page.

Quarter

Momentum. A hard corner that pushes the eye toward the next thing.
