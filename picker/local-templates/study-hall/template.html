STUDY HALL Principles
04 / 13
Five small rules

Five rules for a calmer system

keep it gentle, keep it clear
01

One idea per slide

If it needs a second headline, it needs a second slide.

02

White space is a feature

Let the pastel wash and the margins do the decorating.

03

Speak like a person

Warm verbs, short sentences, no jargon fog.

04

Highlight one thing

A single marker stroke per idea, never three.

05

Keep the rhythm

24px steps, 30px body, 34px and up for headings.

If in doubt, add white space.
