PorcelainPrinciples
04 / 13
Principles

Four rules we
design by.

Every screen in the system can be traced back to one of these four commitments.

01

Let white space lead

Space is the primary material. If a slide feels busy, remove a layer before adding one.

02

Color is structure

Pastel fields mark hierarchy and grouping. They replace borders, not meaning.

03

One ink, four fills

All type and rules stay in the same ink. Color lives in fills, never in text.

04

Quiet motion

Fades and gentle rises only. Movement should feel like breathing, not performing.

PorcelainThe System
05 / 13
The system

A palette that behaves like a grid.

Each color has a job: blush for emphasis, mint for confirmation, butter for attention, periwinkle for context.

Used together, they create rhythm without noise — and keep the page legible at a glance.

  • One ink, four pastel fills
  • Soft radii with hairline rules
  • Never more than two pastels per view
